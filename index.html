Biometrics · Trustworthy AI · Deepfake Detection · Privacy-Preserving Authentication

Md Mashfiq Rizvee

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Kansas, where my research focuses on scalable and trustworthy authentication systems spanning facial biometrics, privacy-preserving identity verification, probabilistic data structures, and hardware security.

My doctoral work develops hierarchical Bloom-filter architectures for fast, noise-tolerant authentication of humans and electronic devices. I am also a Data Engineering Intern at Jumio, where I work on production-oriented biometric verification pipelines, liveness and fraud analysis, and deepfake detection using frequency-domain artifacts.

Focus

Research

My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, computer vision, biometrics, data privacy, probabilistic data structures, and secure data management. I am particularly interested in authentication systems that remain efficient and reliable as databases grow, observations become noisy, and privacy constraints become stronger.

My dissertation, Hierarchical Probabilistic Architectures for Scalable Biometric and Electronic Authentication in Secure Surveillance, develops a unified family of Hierarchical Bloom Filter architectures for biometric and hardware identity systems. This work includes Dynamic Hierarchical Bloom Filters for facial biometrics, Bio-BloomChain for decentralized biometric authentication, and Persistent Hierarchical Bloom Filters for PUF-based electronic authentication.
